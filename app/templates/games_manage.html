Manage Games

The Series folder in your library gets auto-grouped into a "game" for the picker, but that guess doesn't always match how you think about it - e.g. "Savage Rifts" or "The Rifter" might feel like part of "Rifts" to you even though they're a different rules system or cover more than one line. Rename a game, merge one game entirely into another, or move a single Series to a different game below. Changes apply immediately - no rescan needed.
